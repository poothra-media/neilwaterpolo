Privacy policy

Neil Water Polo uploader is a personal tool used only by the owner of the @NeilWaterPolo YouTube channel.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's permission it uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, edit their titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and create live broadcasts on that one channel.

What it stores and shares

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computers. The tool does not collect, sell or share any personal data, and it is not offered to other users.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
